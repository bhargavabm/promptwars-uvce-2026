Privacy Notice

This notice explains, in plain language, how the Bankism demonstration handles your information.

What we collect

When you sign in, we store a display name and either the Google subject identifier from your Google account or the phone number you enter. We also store the time you accepted this notice, if you accepted it.

If you save banking details, we store the account holder name, the bank name, the IFSC code, and only the last four digits of the account number.

Why we collect it

We collect this information only to demonstrate sign-in and a simple profile. It lets the site show your name in the navigation bar, keep a signed-in session, and let you download or delete what is stored about you.

What we do not collect

We do not store your full account number. Account numbers are reduced to their last four digits as soon as they are saved.

We do not store the text you enter on the check page, and we do not keep your messages or screening results.

We do not share any data with third parties. There is no advertising, tracking, or analytics on this site.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only while your account exists. Phone verification codes expire within minutes and are removed after they are used or expire. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, your bank details, and any pending verification code.

Your choices

You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time from the profile page. Deleting your account signs you out and removes the stored rows described above.
